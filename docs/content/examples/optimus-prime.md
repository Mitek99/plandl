title: Residential plan
---

![Grid-based residential plan with five rooms](../optimus-prime.svg)

This larger example combines five rooms, explicit wall defaults, a bearing grid line, furniture, a window, an opening, and an inner door with an inferred utility-side counterpart. `BEARING X=[2]` marks the complete vertical grid line, affecting both the bedroom's outer east wall and inner walls on that line.

The same definition is editable in the [front-page playground](../../#try-plandl). The complete source is available in the repository at `examples/optimus-prime.plandl`.
