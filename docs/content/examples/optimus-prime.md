![Approximate PlanDL conversion of the supplied residential plan](../optimus-prime.svg)

This is an approximate conversion of the supplied `optimus-prime.webp` floor-plan image, not a dimension-exact reconstruction. It preserves the 11,875 × 11,875 mm envelope, the main room arrangement, and the labeled areas to within rounding. Doors, windows, and furniture are representative because the raster image does not provide a complete machine-readable specification.

The conversion uses auto-extension throughout: the west exterior closes the envelope, and internal partitions stop at earlier partitions to form the required T-junctions.

The same definition is editable in the [front-page playground](../../#try-plandl). The complete source is available in the repository at `examples/optimus-prime.plandl`.
